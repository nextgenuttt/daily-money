Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Daily Money is made by Vignesh. Contact: nextgenuttt@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, debts, reminders, tasks and receipt photos are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Receipt photos and bank statements you import are read on the phone (Google ML Kit runs on the device).

Bank SMS (only if you turn it on)

Settings → Find payments in bank SMS is off by default. If you turn it on and allow the SMS permission, the app reads messages from bank and UPI sender IDs (for example AX-HDFCBK) on your phone to find payments: amount, debit or credit, who was paid, the date and the last digits of the account. Messages from people, OTPs and offers are skipped. The message text is not stored; only those payment details are kept on the phone, and nothing is added until you tap Add. Your messages are never uploaded, shared or used for anything else. Turn it off in Settings or remove the permission in your phone's settings at any time. You can also add a payment by sharing a single message into the app, which needs no permission.

What reaches our servers

  • Account: name, email and sign-in provider, through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Groups (only if you use them): group names and members, invitations, and the expenses you choose to share: amount, category, date, note, split and approvals. Never your accounts or balances.
  • Payments between members: settlements you record, your UPI ID and mobile number if you add them (optional; shown only to people in your groups, so they can pay you or send you a payment reminder on WhatsApp/SMS from their own phone), and payment reminders members send you.
  • Messages and notifications about shared expenses.
  • Device: a random device ID, app version and a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Pushes carry no content.

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) and Google Firebase, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

What we don't do

No ads, no selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers, no tracking across apps.

Who sees your shared data

Only members of the group you share with, limited by the group's roles and your "who can see this" choice. Group owners and admins manage members and roles.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your account. Shared expenses stay for the other members, shown as "Former member". Group audit history keeps only an anonymous ID instead of your name.

Children

Daily Money is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

We'll show changes here and in the app before they take effect.
