Delete your Daily Money account

In the app (fastest)

Open Daily Money → Settings → Delete account → Delete for good. If you own a group that still has other members, make someone else the owner first (Groups → the group → Members).

Without the app

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, UPI ID, devices and notifications: deleted at once.
  • Money, reminders, tasks and receipts kept on your phone: deleted from the phone when you delete in the app (or uninstall it). They were never on our servers.
  • You leave all your groups. Expenses you shared stay visible to the other members, shown as "Former member", because their balances depend on them.
  • Group audit history (who changed what) keeps only an anonymous ID instead of your name.

Backups you saved yourself (.dmbackup files) are yours; delete them where you stored them.
